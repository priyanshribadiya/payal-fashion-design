How Ordering Works

◆

Getting your dream outfit is smooth and transparent. Three simple steps connecting you directly with our atelier.

01

Explore the Collection

Browse our ready-made styles above or gather your own outfit photos and ideas from Pinterest, weddings, or magazines.

02

Choose or Describe

Select your favorite design or tell us your custom requirements: outfit type, fabric preference, measurements, and target date.

03

Connect on WhatsApp

Chat directly with Payal Fashion Design to confirm price, measurements, production timeline, and pickup or delivery details.

Note on payments: We do not take automated online card payments on this website. All price confirmations, measurement consultations, and orders are finalized directly with the boutique owner on WhatsApp or in person.
